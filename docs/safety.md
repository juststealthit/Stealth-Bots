Check the handle letter by letter. A look-alike with an extra letter, an underscore or a swapped character isn't us.

## What our bots will never do

- **Ask for your recovery phrase or private key.** Nobody from Just Stealth It ever will.
- **DM you first.** The private bot only answers when you start the chat. The X agent doesn't do DMs at all.
- **Ask you to connect a wallet on another site.** Every link goes to juststealthit.xyz, a chain explorer, or a person's public profile.
- **Run giveaways, airdrops or presales.** The posts are checked so they can't even say so.
- **Tell you to buy, sell or hold.** No price calls, no "100x", no promises.
- **Post in another group.** The community poster lives in t.me/juststealthit and leaves any other group it's added to.

If something claiming to be us does any of these, it isn't us.

## The private bot holds a key

[@juststealthbot](https://t.me/juststealthbot)'s private chat is built and currently off. Once it's on:

- **Just Stealth It holds that wallet's key.** Keep there only what you plan to use.
- You can export the key. It's the one moment the key is shown, and the message deletes itself after about 60 seconds. Never paste it into a site or send it to anyone.
- On the site, a wallet you connect stays yours. You sign its transactions and Just Stealth It never sees its keys.

More in [The private bot](telegram-bot.md) and the platform's [Safety and trust](https://github.com/juststealthit/docs/blob/main/docs/safety.md).

## Bots can be wrong

The bots post facts read from the chain and from the platform. Some text and pictures are made by the Stealth agent, and it can get things wrong.

- Launch, burn and claim posts carry a **TX** button whenever there's a transaction. Check it.
- Every coin post links to its page on juststealthit.xyz. Check the coin there.
- A post about a coin isn't an endorsement. Coins here are launched by anyone.

## Coins aren't vetted by a post

A coin showing up in All Launches only means it launched. It says nothing about who made it or whether it's worth anything. You can lose everything you put in.
