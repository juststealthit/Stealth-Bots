# FAQ

### Where do I see new launches?

In [t.me/juststealthit](https://t.me/juststealthit). Every launch goes to **All Launches** and to its chain's topic: SOL, HOOD, BASE, ARC, BSC or HYPE. See [The Telegram group](telegram-group.md).

### Why isn't my launch on X?

Launches aren't posted on X. They go to Telegram and the Feed. A graduation or a marketing step does get an X post. See [X posts](x-posts.md).

### My coin launched but I don't see a post.

A few reasons:

- On a busy day, launches in one topic fold into a digest. Look for "🚀 N new launches".
- An event that reaches the bot late isn't posted hours after. It's still on the Feed.
- A coin hidden by moderation isn't posted.

### Can I pay to be posted, pinned or boosted by the bots?

No. The bots post what happens on chain and on the platform. A coin on the Marketing launch type pays for its marketing from its own trading fees. See the [marketing ladder](https://github.com/juststealthit/docs/blob/main/docs/marketing.md).

### Why are only $STEALTH burns in BUYBACKS?

BUYBACKS is the topic for the buy-back and burn of $STEALTH. Coins on the Buy back and burn launch type burn their own supply too, and those burns show on the coin's page and the Feed.

### Why does a burn sometimes post a little after its buy?

A buy-back is always posted buy first, then burn. If the burn is seen first, it waits a short while for its buy.

### Is the private bot live?

Not yet. It's built and currently off. Launch from [juststealthit.xyz](https://juststealthit.xyz) for now. See [The private bot](telegram-bot.md).

### Who holds the private bot's wallet key?

Just Stealth It does, and signs when you confirm a launch or a withdrawal. You can export the key. Keep there only what you plan to use.

### Can the private bot launch on EVM chains?

Not yet. In the bot they show **soon**. The site launches on all of them today.

### Who is Stealth?

Stealth is the X agent of Just Stealth It. It replies from [@juststealthitai](https://x.com/juststealthitai). It's automated, and says so if you ask. See [The X agent](x-agent.md).

### Why didn't Stealth answer me?

Most mentions don't get a reply. It answers only real questions about a coin on the platform, the marketing, the platform itself, or who it is. You also need to mention @juststealthitai yourself. It skips praise, DM requests, offers, scam bait and price questions. A thread gets up to 5 replies.

### Will Stealth tell me if a coin is going up?

No. It never talks about where a price is going, and never tells anyone to buy, sell or hold.

### Stealth said something wrong.

It can happen. Its numbers come from live data, but the words are generated. Check the coin's page on juststealthit.xyz, and tell us through the form on [juststealthit.xyz/status](https://juststealthit.xyz/status).

### Someone DMed me as Just Stealth It.

It isn't us. Our bots never DM first and never ask for a key or recovery phrase. See [Staying safe](safety.md).

### Is there a bot for alerts on my coins?

Not yet. Per-coin subscriptions, price alerts and wallet alerts are [planned](roadmap.md).

### Are the bots in other languages?

English for now. More languages are [planned](roadmap.md). The site itself is in English, 简体中文, 日本語, Español and Français.

### Where do I get help?

The form on [juststealthit.xyz/status](https://juststealthit.xyz/status). The bots aren't support.
